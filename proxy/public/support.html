Vino Cellar Support

Keep track of what's in your cellar, what it's worth, and when to drink it.

Need help? Email info@orangeeaglesa.com.

Please include your app version and device ID — both are at the bottom of Settings, and together they explain most problems in one reply.

Getting started

Tap + on the Cellar tab to add a bottle. You can scan the label with the camera, pick a photo, or type the details in. Set how many bottles you have, what you paid, and where they're stored; the app totals the value of the whole cellar for you.

Common questions

The app didn't read my label correctly

Scanning works best on a flat, well-lit label with the text roughly horizontal. Whatever it reads goes into the form as editable text, so you can correct it before saving. On the simulator, or a device without a camera, use the photo picker instead.

Your phone reads the characters and the reading service sorts them into the right fields, repairing the usual OCR damage along the way. If a label defeats it — ornate script, embossing, a torn label — the form offers Read the photo instead. That is the only time a label photo leaves your phone, and only because you tapped it.

Why doesn't my wine have a price?

Price estimates are optional and come from a pricing service configured in Settings. Without one, you can type your own estimate per 750 mL and every total still works. With one, a lookup runs when you add a wine, and results are cached for seven days — tap Refresh Prices under the cellar value to force a new one.

It says I've used up my price lookups

The pricing service limits how many lookups each install can make, so one person can't exhaust the allowance for everyone. Prices already fetched keep working, and the limit resets. If you need more, get in touch.

What is LWIN?

LWIN is Liv-ex's free wine identity database, bundled with the app (around 205,000 wines and spirits). Matching a wine to its LWIN code gives it a canonical identity, which makes price lookups far more accurate. It's optional — the app suggests a match and you confirm it.

Where did my bottle go?

When the last bottle of a wine is marked Consumed, the wine moves to the Drank tab, keeping its notes, rating and photo. Swipe it there to put it back in the cellar if you buy another, or onto the wishlist.

Can I keep bottles in more than one place?

Yes — create collections (Home, the beach house, a storage locker) and assign bottles to them. The Value tab totals each collection separately.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. The Value tab's share button exports your whole cellar as CSV or as a PDF summary. Your data is yours, and it never leaves your phone unless you send it somewhere.

Drink-window reminders

Give a bottle a drink-from and drink-to year and the app schedules a local notification when it enters its window. Allow notifications the first time you're asked; you can change that later in iOS Settings.

Privacy

Your cellar never leaves your device. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. See the privacy policy for the details, including exactly what a price lookup sends.
