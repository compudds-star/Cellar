Privacy Policy

Vino Cellar for iPhone · last updated 22 September 2026

Your cellar stays on your phone. Vino Cellar has no account to create and no servers holding your collection. Your wines, bottles, photos, tasting notes and ratings are stored only on your device.

There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDK of any kind in the app.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter or capture: wines and bottles, quantities, prices paid, storage locations, drink windows, tasting notes, ratings, wishlist, collections, and any label photos you scan or choose. It lives in the app's own database, protected by iOS file encryption, and is included in your device backup if you back your iPhone up.

Deleting the app deletes that database.

What leaves your device, and only if you ask for prices

Online price estimates are optional. If a pricing service is configured (Settings → Pricing endpoint), then when you add a wine or tap Refresh Prices the app sends that service:

  • the wine's producer, name and vintage, and its LWIN identifier if matched;
  • the currency you want prices in;
  • a random identifier for your installation, described below.

It does not send your cellar, your quantities, your prices paid, your notes, your ratings or your location, and it does not send a photo unless you ask it to read one. Clear the endpoint in Settings and nothing leaves the device at all — the rest of the app keeps working.

The installation identifier

Each install generates a short random code such as Ryc#j0, stored in your device's keychain and sent with price lookups. It exists so whoever runs the pricing service can meter usage and stop one install from exhausting a paid API allowance for everyone.

It is not derived from your device, your Apple Account or anything about you, and it is not linked to a name, an email address or any other identifier. The pricing service records only that code, a count of lookups, and when it was last seen. Note that because it lives in the keychain, it survives deleting and reinstalling the app; if you want the record removed, contact us and we'll delete it.

Camera, photos and location

  • Camera and photo library — used only to read a wine label. Text recognition runs on your device. The text it recognises is sent to the reading service to be sorted into producer, name and vintage, because OCR output is often garbled and out of order. The photo itself stays on your phone unless you tap “Read the photo instead”, which is offered only when the text was too damaged to identify the bottle. Nothing is sent automatically, and neither the text nor the photo is stored by us.
  • Location — requested only when you ask for wine shops near you. It is used to run a map search through Apple Maps and is never stored by us or sent to our servers. Apple's handling of that search is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing into the app

When you share a page, link or photo to Vino Cellar from another app, that content is written to a private folder shared between the app and its share extension, and imported the next time you open the app. It is not transmitted anywhere.

Exports and notifications

CSV and PDF exports are generated on your device and go wherever you choose to send them. Drink-window reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; nothing about them is transmitted.

Children

Vino Cellar concerns alcoholic beverages and is not directed at children. It collects nothing that would identify a person of any age.

Data we sell or share

None. There is nobody to sell it to — we do not hold your data. The pricing service's provider receives only the wine name and vintage needed to answer a price query.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be described in the app's release notes.
